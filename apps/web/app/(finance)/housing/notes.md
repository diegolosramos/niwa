# Real Cost of Ownership Equation

- t = tax rate
- r = repair rate
- i = down payment percentage
- y = opportunity cost rate
- q = S&P 500 average return percentage
- a = median home appreciation rate
- d = mortgage rate

- T = Tax
- R = Maintenance
- I = Down Payment
- Y = Opportunity Cost
- D = Cost of Debt
- P = House price
- K = True Cost of Ownership
- N = True Monthly Cost of Ownership


y = q - a

T = t * P
R = r * P
I = i * P
Y = y * I
D = d * (P - I)

K = T + R + Y + D
N = K / 12

# Glossary

Tax rate (t): The percentage of the house price that is paid as property tax.
Repair rate (r): The percentage of the house price that is spent on maintenance and repairs.
Down payment percentage (i): The portion of the house price paid upfront.
Opportunity cost rate (y): The difference between the S&P 500 average return and the median home appreciation rate.
S&P 500 average return percentage (q): The historical average annual return of the S&P 500.
Median home appreciation rate (a): The typical annual increase in home value.
Mortgage rate (d): The interest rate on the mortgage loan.
True Cost of Ownership (K): The total annual cost of owning the house, including taxes, maintenance, opportunity cost, and debt.
True Monthly Cost of Ownership (N): The total monthly cost of owning the house.
